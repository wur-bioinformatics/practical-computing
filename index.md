


## Lab journal
We highly recommend keeping a lab journal to record your exercise work:
- What did you do?
- Specify URLs, programs, settings, etc.
- What were the results?
You can simply use a text file, for instance a Word or Google docs document. You
can also use more advanced software, like Microsoft OneNote, or Obsidian.
It is a time investment which will pay off by solving the same problem twice or
having to reconstruct what you did two weeks ago
