## Introduction
Biopython is a collection of tools that makes it easy to work with biological data in Python. General Python is excellent for programming, but it doesn't understand DNA or proteins. Biopython provides specialized tools for molecular biology and bioinformatics.
It is one of the oldest and most widely used bioinformatics libraries. It is used in research institutes, hospitals, universities, and biotech companies.
